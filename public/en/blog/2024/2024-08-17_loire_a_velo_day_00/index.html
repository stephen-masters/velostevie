60km from Nevers to Pouilly-sur-Loire

La Loire à Vélo - Day 1 - Nevers to Pouilly-sur-Loire  

Today was the first day of my La Loire à Vélo itinerary, taking me 60km from Nevers to Pouilly-sur-Loire.

The day began with a little wander around Nevers itself. It is well worth taking some time to explore the town, see the medieval buildings and palaces, and maybe visit the Musée de la Faïence et des Beaux-Arts to learn about that local style of ceramics.

Leaving Nevers on the La Loire à Vélo was a special experience, as I began by crossing this wild part of the Loire river. There’s something about leaving the town and seeing this expanse of river that gives a taste of what is to come over the coming days.

The first of the day’s riding was spent following the canal towpath from Nevers to Cuffy.

Near Cuffy is the spectacular Bec d’Alier, where the river Alier joins the Loire.

Maybe around 40km into the ride, I found this cute little cafe where I was able to rest for a short while and have a cold drink.

I detoured into La Charité-sur-Loire, a medieval town which is famed as a book town, packed with small bookshops.

My day ended at Le Coq Hardi, a small hotel in Pouilly-sur-Loire.

The garden has a lovely patio looking down towards the Loire with a vegetable garden at the side where they grow tomatoes, pumpkins and plenty more.

The hotel is in a fantastic location on a hill overlooking the river. Across the road is the tasting room of a local winery, so if you get there in time, you might be able to treat yourself to a wine tasting. I arrived too late for that! The food is really special here, so I had to take photos of all the courses I ate. As a meal at the end of my first day of riding, it left me hoping that I should be so lucky for more meals on this trip. When you’re tired from a day of riding, a delicious meal with a glass of local wine really hits the spot.
